Raila Amolo Odinga

Champion of Democracy and Former Prime Minister of Kenya

Timeline & Legacy

  • 1945: Born in Maseno, Kisumu District, Kenya.
  • 1970s: Educated as a mechanical engineer in East Germany before returning to lecture at the University of Nairobi.
  • 1980s: Imprisoned for political activism and advocacy for multiparty democracy in Kenya.
  • 1992: Elected Member of Parliament for Lang'ata constituency following the return of multiparty elections.
  • 2001 - 2002: Served as Minister for Energy and played a key role in the historic NARC coalition victory.
  • 2008 - 2013: Appointed Prime Minister of Kenya under the Coalition Government following the 2007 election framework.
  • 2010: Championed the promulgation and adoption of Kenya's landmark 2010 Constitution.
  • 2018: Initiated the "Handshake" reconciliation framework to foster political unity and stability.
